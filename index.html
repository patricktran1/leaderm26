Why LEADderm exists

The “missing curriculum” around medicine.

LEADderm was created around a simple gap: clinical mastery is essential, but modern medicine also demands leadership, empathy, innovation, communication and a strong professional community.

Leadership

Practical frameworks for leading effectively, fostering innovation and driving positive change within dermatology.

Community

A network designed for connection, collaboration and a stronger sense of belonging across the specialty.

Diversity

Perspectives and initiatives that strengthen patient care by expanding who is heard, represented and supported.

Career

Workshops, peer learning and relationships that help physicians navigate advancement with more intention.

Well-being

Tools for balance, resilience and sustainable performance inside a demanding profession.

“Better leadership leads to better health.”LEADderm foundational principle
