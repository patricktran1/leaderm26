Conference context

The missing curriculum around clinical expertise.

LEADderm presents itself as a different kind of dermatology meeting, focused on leadership, community, diversity, career development and well-being alongside the professional network around dermatology.

The stronger digital product is not merely a prettier event page. It is a useful layer for the weekend: fast agenda access, faculty discovery, post-session resources, an evolving media archive and eventually an AI concierge that understands the program.
